## What I do
Critical Systems Support Specialist at FedEx (Special Assignment, Wave 1). I support the
routing systems that decide how packages move through the network, the address-cleansing
services that feed them, and the FORGE handheld scanners couriers carry. Issues reach me by
email, phone, and in person; what I can't resolve directly becomes a ServiceNow ticket with
reproduction steps and diagnostics. I travel for network integration launches — six so far,
across CA, AZ, TX, TN, and OH.

Alongside that: sole technical support for a single-principal real estate office (part-time,
2016–2024), where every hardware, software, network, and data problem routed to me — including
pulling live QuickBooks data off a failing Windows XP machine by mounting its drive in a VM on
modern hardware. Before that, a public access station (2016–2020), where I picked up the
station's IT operations after the IT manager left and kept the broadcast server and Proxmox
hypervisor running from his runbooks until the station closed.

Same job either way: someone can't do their work, the cause has to be found, and what was done
about it has to be written down.
